How it works

  1. A customer fills in your web form or texts your number.
  2. They get a text back in seconds.
  3. You get their details by text; the ready ones are handed to you.
  4. Every enquiry lands in your Client CRM, with the full conversation.
  5. You mark each one done, booked or lost, with one tap in the CRM or one word by text, and set its job value and follow-up.
  6. A 9:00 text lists what is open.

Packages

  1. Instant Reply

    Every enquiry gets a text back in seconds saying you received it and will be in touch. You get the customer's details by text and take it from there.

  2. Lead Assistant

    Everything in Instant Reply, plus the system answers the customer's questions by text from facts you give it (hours, service area, what you offer, what you need from them). When a customer has given their details and wants to go ahead, you get a text with everything and you take over to book them.

  3. Instant Quote

    Everything in Lead Assistant, plus the customer gets a priced quote by text in seconds, worked out from your one-page price list.

Each package covers up to 100 enquiries a month. An enquiry is one customer who fills in your form or texts your number. More than that is priced on the call. Which one fits is decided on a 20-minute call, from how you quote today and how many enquiries you get.

Client CRM

About

We believe a business should be designed before it gets busy.

An architect draws the structure first, so that nothing is left to chance once the building begins. We try to work the same way.

Small business owners are asked to do a great deal: the work, the customers, the paperwork, the follow-through. They should not also lose work to bad timing or a poor memory. We think calm, reliable systems beat hustle, and that a missed moment or a forgotten note should never decide what happens next.

So we start with the plan. What we build is kept simple enough to understand, honest about its limits, and steady enough to forget about.

We write down what we learn, with the numbers and their limits, in plain words.

Design the structure first. The rest is just building.
