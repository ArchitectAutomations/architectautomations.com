How it works

  1. A customer fills in your web form or texts your number.
  2. They get a text back in seconds.
  3. You get their details by text; the ready ones are handed to you.
  4. You answer with one word: DONE, BOOKED or LOST.
  5. A 9:00 text lists what is open.

Packages

  1. Instant Reply

    Every enquiry gets a text back in seconds saying you received it and will be in touch. You get the customer's details by text and take it from there.

  2. Lead Assistant

    Everything in Instant Reply, plus the system answers the customer's questions by text from facts you give it (hours, service area, what you offer, what you need from them). When a customer has given their details and wants to go ahead, you get a text with everything and you take over to book them.

  3. Instant Quote

    Everything in Lead Assistant, plus the customer gets a priced quote by text in seconds, worked out from your one-page price list.

Each package covers up to 100 enquiries a month. An enquiry is one customer who fills in your form or texts your number. More than that is priced on the call. Which one fits is decided on a 20-minute call, from how you quote today and how many enquiries you get. About 60 a month suits Instant Quote (assumes 1 in 10 book; unmeasured), about 35 Lead Assistant, fewer Instant Reply.

Who it is for

Owner-run businesses in Canada that take enquiries by web form or text, where speed decides who wins. For example:

  • Mobile bars

  • House cleaners

Who it is not for

  • Franchises and multi-location brands.
  • Anyone already using an auto-responder or chatbot.
  • Law, medical or finance intake.
  • Quebec, for now.
  • Instagram-only or missed-call-only enquiries (not covered).
  • Age-restricted products (carriers block those texts).
